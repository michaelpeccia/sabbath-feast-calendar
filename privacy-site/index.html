Privacy Policy for the Sabbath & Feast Days Calendar

Effective October 27, 2026

In short: the app does not send any of your personal information to us or anyone else. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app calculates and saves stays on your phone.

Who we are

The Sabbath & Feast Days Calendar ("the app") is made by Kingdom Life Ministry. You can reach us at michaelbenyah@kingdom-life-ministry.com.

Your location

If you tap "Use my location," the app asks Android for permission to read your phone's location. It uses your location only to calculate sunset times and feast times where you are. Your location is saved in the app's storage on your phone so it does not have to ask again. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

You do not have to share your location. You can type in a location by hand instead. You can turn off location permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

What the app saves on your phone

The app saves these on your phone only:

  • Your location, if you set one
  • Your calendar and name choices
  • New moon sightings and aviv choices you mark
  • Your prep checklists
  • Your reminder choices
  • The date your free trial started, and whether the app is unlocked
  • Upcoming Sabbath and feast times for the home-screen widget

We cannot see any of this. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's Settings.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android. They are not sent from a server, and turning them on does not share anything with us. You can change or turn off reminders in the app's Settings, or turn off notifications for the app in your phone's Settings.

Internet use

The app works offline. It only uses the internet when you tap a link in the app, such as the link to the Kingdom Life Study app. Links open in your web browser, and the website you visit has its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The app is free to download with a 14-day free trial. The trial start date is saved on your phone only. To keep using the app after the trial, you can unlock it with a one-time purchase on Google Play. The app checks with Google Play on your phone whether you have bought the unlock, and nothing about this is sent to us.

When you buy the unlock on Google Play, Google handles your payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your card or bank details. Google may share basic order details with us, such as an order number, so we can help with refunds and support.

If you buy the app directly from Kingdom Life Ministry, the payment service you choose handles your payment under its own policy. We use your contact details only to send you the app and help you with it, and we never sell or share them.

Children

The app is a calendar and Scripture reference for general audiences. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: michaelbenyah@kingdom-life-ministry.com
